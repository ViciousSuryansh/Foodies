Frequently Asked Questions

~Is Foodies Bread really baked fresh each day?

Many commercial bakeries and large supermarket-style “in-store bakeries” do not make bread completely from scratch each day. Instead, they use a method called parbaking (or frozen-dough / part-baked dough). In this method: dough is prepared and partially baked (or sometimes frozen as raw dough) elsewhere; then transported; and the final baking (or re-baking) happens “in store.”

~Do you bake breads containing animal fats or products?

We do not bake breads using animal fats, lard, eggs, or dairy. All of our breads are made with plant-based ingredients such as flour, water, yeast, salt, and vegetable oils. This makes our bread suitable for vegetarians, vegans, and anyone looking to avoid animal-derived products.

~Can I order you products online?

There are ready‑made tools and systems (for example, systems like Vev or chotu) that let a bakery create a simple online storefront where customers can browse items, place orders, and choose delivery or pickup.
